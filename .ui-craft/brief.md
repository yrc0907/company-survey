# Research Workbench Design Brief

## Product identity

- Product: personal research workbench for company, industry, competitor and policy reports.
- Audience: one analyst who needs to read sources, compare claims and revise reports quickly.
- Product language: calm, precise, evidence-first, no decorative dashboard behavior.

## Visual intent

- Style: minimal clean research desk, not a marketing landing page and not a generic chat clone.
- Theme: porcelain white with graphite text and a restrained cobalt accent.
- Density: 7/10. The report and sources are the primary content, not metric cards.
- Motion: 2/10. Short hover and panel transitions only; honor reduced motion.
- Variance: 4/10. Stable three-column shell with one distinctive command palette interaction.

## Audience decision

Within 60 seconds, the user should be able to answer: what report am I reading, what source supports a claim, what is still unverified, and what should I edit next.

## Learned constraints

- Keep the interface advanced, restrained and coherent; avoid card grids, gradients and decorative dark panels.
- Use white/near-white surfaces, deep neutral type and a small amount of blue only for focus and primary actions.
- AI must appear as an evidence-aware right-side tool, never as the entire product.
